Bring color into your home I love experimenting with colors, shapes, textures, and materials. For my monoprints, I use natural materials like leaves and grasses, but also everyday objects of all kinds, even empty packaging materials. This range makes the results so diverse and unique. The only constant is the Gelli Plate itself, with its dimensions. This ensures that each of my works fits into one of the square frames, allowing for a variety of rotating exhibitions depending on the season, my mood, and my inspiration.
